Often, we believe the best way to do something is the way we've always done it. But usually, that's just because we haven't been introduced to a more efficient method. We get comfortable and lazy, and better alternatives simply never cross our minds.
Every day, you probably open several applications to get your work done. But very quickly, they start overlapping. They stack on top of each other, getting lost behind one another. You freely drag one window here and another there, but let's be honest: it gets messy and disorganized.
